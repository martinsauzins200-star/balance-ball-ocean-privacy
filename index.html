Balance Ball Ocean

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026. Applies to the Android game Balance Ball Ocean (initial Google Play testing release 2.8.2).

This Privacy Policy describes how Balance Ball Ocean, an independent mobile game, accesses, uses, stores, and shares information. This description is specific to the current Android release. Features and practices may change in future updates, in which case this page will be updated.

1. Information used by the game

Motion sensors. The game uses your device's accelerometer to detect how you tilt the phone, calibrate your controls, and move the ball. Accelerometer readings are processed on your device to run the game. We do not intentionally transmit those readings to a game server.

Locally stored game data. The game can store local gameplay progress, unlocked levels, scores, and optional in-game name or preferences in the application's local storage. These values are used to resume gameplay and display your progress on the device. The current release does not require creating an online account.

2. Advertising and third-party components

The current Google Play release does not display advertisements or request ad impressions. The Android package includes Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform components prepared for possible future versions; advertising functionality is disabled in the current release build. Before any production advertising is enabled, this policy and our Google Play data disclosures will be reviewed and updated to account for the advertising providers' practices and applicable consent requirements.

Android and Google Play services may separately process information as part of downloading, installing, securing, or operating apps under their own applicable terms. This policy does not replace those providers' privacy notices.

3. Online services and sharing

The game levels and graphics are bundled in the Android application. The game can be played offline. The current release does not connect to a public online leaderboard or intentionally upload gameplay scores to a central game server. We do not intentionally share locally stored scores, tilt readings or gameplay progress with third parties. We do not sell these records.

The app declares Android internet and network-state permissions to support potential future online features. The presence of these permissions does not mean that an online leaderboard is active.

4. Security

The game uses Android app-specific storage to keep local gameplay data. We do not maintain a remote account database for this release. Please keep your device updated and protect access to your phone. If an online service is introduced later, we will document its security practices here.

5. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the application's storage or uninstall the game, subject to how Android manages backups and app storage. You can remove local game data through Android Settings > Apps > Balance Ball Ocean > Storage > Clear data. Because this release has no online game accounts and does not store your gameplay data on our servers, there is no corresponding online game account to delete.

6. Children's privacy

This game does not currently ask for an account or collect a child's name as a verified identity. Parents and guardians can supervise children's use of the game. If we later add advertising, online rankings, or new data collection, we will review applicable children's privacy and Google Play Families requirements before release.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when gameplay, online services, advertising, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted at this same address with a revised date.
